# Historical Transport Movement Restriction Reports

The reports below are historical. Do not tell passengers that these restrictions
are currently in force without confirmation from the relevant authorities.

## 2024 report

BusinessDay reported that Lafia LGA announced night movement restrictions
reported effective 3 June 2024:

- Motorcycles: 6:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
- Tricycles: 8:00 PM to 6:00 AM.

Source: BusinessDay, 4 June 2024, research reference `turn279590search8`.

## 2025 report

Infoland reported a police clarification stating:

- There was no general curfew in Lafia.
- Night movement restrictions applied to motorcycles and tricycles.
- The report did not establish a definitive current 2026 restriction schedule.

Source: Infoland, 12 May 2025, research reference `turn279590search4`.
