# Yahaya Sabo Bus Terminal

Yahaya Sabo Bus Terminal is associated with Bukan Sidi, Lafia.

**Evidence status: Government-referenced distribution-point listing (2024).**

The Federal Ministry of Information listed Yahaya Sabo Bus Terminal as a Lafia
distribution point in its 2024 road-transport palliative information. This
government listing is evidence of a distribution point; it does not establish
current daily passenger operations.

**Evidence status: Operator-published website observation (4 November 2026).**

Victoria Travels lists its Lafia station at Yahaya Sabo Bus Terminal and lists
Abuja–Lafia and Makurdi–Lafia routes. The operator's route listing does not
establish current departure times or fares.

## Passenger information limitation

Live schedules and fares require direct confirmation with the operator. Do not
infer current passenger operations from the government distribution-point
listing.
